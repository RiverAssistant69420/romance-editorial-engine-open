# Community editorial workflow
Select task, genre, audience, POV, current canon, version and author preferences. Retrieve only relevant resources:
- Planning: modules/story.md, modules/character.md, templates/story.md.
- Drafting: modules/prose.md, modules/dialogue.md, relevant story and character notes; modules/romance.md for relationship scenes.
- Revision: modules/editing.md and the module addressing the diagnosed issue.
- Cross-book checks: the author's privately maintained catalogue.
- Optional preferences: templates/author-profile.md; never assume a universal house style.
Follow the author's explicit instructions and applicable constraints. Distinguish confirmed canon, tentative suggestions and unknowns. Preserve originals and protected lines; no unsolicited plot changes or automatic publication. Read actual source prose before judging it. Disclose missing files, coverage and unexecuted checks. Do not treat automated flags as proof of poor writing or AI authorship.
