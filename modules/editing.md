# Editorial review
Intake: identify version, actual coverage, requested scope, protected elements and applicable style guide. Separate structural, emotional, continuity, prose and mechanical issues.
Developmental: review causality, stakes, character arcs, romance progression and ending. Scene-level: examine viewpoint, physical clarity, dialogue attribution, narrative distance, emotional consequences and pacing. Line edit: review repetition, rhythm, specificity and awkward constructions while protecting voice. Copyedit: apply the author's selected punctuation, spelling and style conventions; distinguish deliberate deviations from errors.
Use mechanical reports as candidate lists, not final verdicts. Compare revisions to original for lost facts, scenes, voice or approved lines. Recheck adjacent transitions and downstream callbacks. For full-manuscript approval, inspect the complete manuscript or state exactly which portions remain unreviewed. Record unresolved issues and never certify an unread text.
