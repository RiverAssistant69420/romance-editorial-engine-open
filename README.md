# Romance Editorial Engine — Community Edition

A general-purpose, customizable framework for planning, drafting, and editing romance fiction with an AI assistant. It is not a model, a writing service, or an automated publishing system.

## Quick start

Ask your assistant to read `AGENTS.md` and the relevant modules in this repository before working on your book. Supply your own outline, manuscript, preferences and approved examples. If repository access is unavailable, download the files and attach the relevant ones to your conversation.

Keep your manuscripts, author profiles and project-specific notes in a separate private workspace. Never commit sensitive or copyrighted source material to this public repository.

## Resources

- `AGENTS.md`: task routing and editorial workflow
- `modules/`: craft guidance by task
- `templates/`: blank author calibration, story and editorial records
- `tools/`: optional local manuscript audit

The framework is advisory: it cannot guarantee literary quality, originality or publication readiness. Authors retain creative responsibility and final approval. Review any AI-assisted text against applicable platform requirements. No author-specific material or external private resources are required.
